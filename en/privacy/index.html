Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026 · Applies to FocusHide

How We Handle Data

FocusHide helps you create local rules to hide apps on a schedule. Rule names, times, repeat days, enabled state, and app tokens returned by the system picker are stored on your device to hide and restore apps on time.

Screen Time Permission

To select and hide apps, FocusHide requests Apple Screen Time authorization and uses system capabilities such as Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings. We do not obtain the real bundle identifiers, icon files, or full usage history of selected apps.

Local Storage

This version does not offer account sign-in, cloud sync, or server backups. Your rule data is stored on this device and in the App Group container for the main app and the monitoring extension. After you delete the app, local data may be removed by the system.

No Sale or Sharing

FocusHide does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law or to respond to feedback you initiate.
