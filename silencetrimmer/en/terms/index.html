Terms of Use

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Applies to Silence Trimmer

Service Description

Silence Trimmer is an iOS app that imports audio on this device, removes long silent gaps, and exports a new m4a file. It does not offer cloud processing, transcription, noise reduction, or video editing. Import is locked until you purchase the unlock. After unlock, import, processing, and export run on this device.

Your Responsibility

You must have the right to process any audio you import, and you should preview the result before exporting. Silence detection is based on audio energy and varies with recording quality, background noise, and the Gentle, Standard, or Aggressive preset. Do not use this app on content you are not allowed to edit or distribute.

File Handling

The app never modifies the original imported file. It creates a new file instead. Export uses the system share sheet, and you choose where the file is saved. Processing may fail if storage is insufficient or if the system blocks the operation.

Purchase Information

Silence Trimmer uses a one-time unlock, not a subscription. After purchase, import, processing, and export remain available. Purchases, restore, refunds, and purchase eligibility are handled under Apple App Store policies. You can restore the purchase on eligible devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Disclaimer

We try to trim silence on-device, but we do not guarantee the result you expect on every recording, device, or system version. Preview before you share. This app is not a professional audio workstation and is not intended as the sole tool for medical, legal, safety, or other high-risk uses.
