Trial Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Applies to Trial Radar

What Trial Radar processes

When you choose a trial confirmation screenshot, Apple Vision recognizes text on your device to suggest a service name, renewal date, and price. You review the suggestions before saving. Trial Radar does not upload or retain the screenshot.

Local information and notifications

Service names, renewal dates and times, and optional prices you save are stored on your device. Trial Radar schedules local notifications through iOS. Notification delivery depends on your iOS settings and device state.

Free-use marker

A small marker recording whether this device has used its one free reminder is stored in the device-only Keychain. It is not synced through iCloud Keychain and may remain after you remove the app so reinstalling does not restore the free reminder.

Purchases and Apple services

If you buy the optional lifetime unlock or use Restore Purchase, StoreKit communicates with Apple to process the transaction and check purchase entitlement. Apple handles payment and account information; Trial Radar does not receive your payment card details. The app can open Apple’s subscription-management page, but does not read your subscriptions or cancel them.

Sharing, analytics, and tracking

Trial Radar does not operate an account or server for reminder data, and this release does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app does not send your screenshot or reminder details to the developer or other companies.

Your choices and retention

You can delete reminders in the app and manage Photos and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Reminder details are stored locally and are removed when you delete them or remove the app. The Keychain free-use marker may persist after app removal as described above.

Children and policy changes

Trial Radar is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect information from children. If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated for the changed release.
