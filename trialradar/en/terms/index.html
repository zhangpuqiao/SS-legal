About the app

Trial Radar helps you record a trial or subscription renewal date, review details recognized from a screenshot, and schedule a local reminder. It does not connect to subscription providers, verify subscription status, or cancel subscriptions.

Free reminder and lifetime unlock

Each device may create one free reminder total. Deleting that reminder or letting it expire does not restore the free allowance. An optional non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks unlimited reminders on devices where your Apple Account entitlement is available. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription; the price shown by the App Store before confirmation applies. Use Restore Purchase with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Recognition and reminders

Screenshot recognition is an aid and may misread a service, date, or price. Check and correct every field before saving. Notifications are scheduled locally by iOS and can be delayed or not delivered because of system settings, Focus, device state, or other factors. Trial Radar cannot guarantee that a reminder will arrive at a particular time. You remain responsible for reviewing and cancelling subscriptions with the provider before renewal.
